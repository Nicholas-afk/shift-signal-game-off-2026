THE SHORT VERSION

Make a little space.

Each solid shape is a signal. Its matching outline is home. Move every signal onto its own outline to finish a frequency.

  1. Use the arrows along the edges to shift a whole row or column by one cell. On a small screen, tap a cell, then use the four arrows below the board.
  2. Signals that leave one edge reappear on the opposite edge. The outline homes stay fixed.
  3. Several signals can move at once. A signal already at home may need to leave before everything fits.
Keyboard

Tab to a cell, or click one. Arrow keys shift its row or column. Hold Shift + an arrow to choose a different cell without moving a signal. Z undoes; R resets. Escape closes a dialog.

Ten frequencies. Optional hints. Take all the time you need. Your completed levels and best move counts are saved on this device when browser storage is available.
