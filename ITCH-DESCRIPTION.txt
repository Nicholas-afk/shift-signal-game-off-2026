SHIFT SIGNAL
A little movement. A different way through.

Shift entire rows and columns to guide solid signals onto their matching outline homes. Signals wrap around the board; one small movement can change the route for everyone.

Ten original spatial puzzles, from a first connection to a five-by-five field of crossed frequencies. No timer. Unlimited undo. Optional shortest-route hints. Quiet procedural sound, off until you choose it.

Play with mouse, touch or keyboard. On a small screen, tap a cell and use the four arrows beneath the board. Keyboard arrows shift the selected cell's line; Shift+arrow selects a different cell. Z undoes, R resets.

Made for Game Off 2026 and the theme SHIFT.
